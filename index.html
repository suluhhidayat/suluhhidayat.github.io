About

Bridging business, systems, and the classroom

I help organizations turn complex business processes into systems people actually use.

Over 6+ years, I've grown from software engineering into system analysis, business analysis, and project management, delivering ERP, HRIS, WMS, Asset Management, and POS solutions for manufacturing, government, banking, retail, and e-commerce clients. My strength is bridging both sides of a project: translating business needs into clear requirements, then keeping scope, timelines, and stakeholders aligned through UAT, training, and go-live.

My Master's in Information Technology, focused on Enterprise Architecture and IT Governance, grounds that work in strategy. Research on aligning information security standards (Indeks KAMI, ISO 27001, NIST CSF), along with hands-on experience as an ISO 9001 & ISO 27001 Internal Auditor, shapes how I approach governance, risk, and quality.

I'm passionate about bridging industry and academia. With experience as a practitioner lecturer (Dosen Praktisi), I aim to pursue a career in higher education, bringing real-world project experience into the classroom. I also mentor professionals and students from diverse career paths through 1-on-1 sessions on CV review, interview preparation, and showcasing their strengths, and I'm always open to discussions on project management and business analysis.
